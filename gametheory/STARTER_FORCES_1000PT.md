## BATTLE FORCE 1: THE IRON VANGUARD OF SOLIS
**Grand Faction:** The Grand Concordat (Crown of Solis)  
**Playstyle:** Disciplined firepower, impenetrable high-iron armor, devastating counter-charges, and defensive battlefield control.  
**Faction Army Rule — [DISCIPLINED BULWARK]:**  
* *Shield Wall:* While a friendly unit is within 3" of another friendly unit, both units reroll natural 1s on Save rolls (subject to the 3+ Save Cap).  
* *Relentless Salvo:* When this army takes the Aimed Fire action, ranged weapons with the `[HAILFIRE]` keyword double their attacks at up to 15" (instead of standard half-range).

## 3. PLAYTEST COMBAT SIMULATION (ROUND 1 CLASH WALKTHROUGH)

To illustrate the seamless, rapid flow of the flat-wound 3-step rules, observe an actual engagement between these two forces:

### STEP 1: SOLIS INITIATIVE & AIMED FIRE
* **Initiative:** Solis wins the roll-off and activates **Crusader Battle Squad Alpha**.
* **Action 1:** `ADVANCE` 6" behind a ruined stone wall (`[HEAVY COVER]`).
* **Action 2:** `AIMED FIRE` at the advancing **Gore-Fiend Reavers Alpha** across the open plaza (18" away).
* **Dice Rolling (Zero Questions Asked to Opponent):**
  * **To-Hit:** 4 Bolters fire 8 shots. Attacker checks **RS 3+** (gains +1 for Aimed Fire = hits on 2+). Squad rolls: 2, 2, 3, 4, 5, 5, 6, 1 (7 Hits).
  * **To-Wound:** Attacker rolls all 7 hits against the Bolter's flat **Wound 4+**. Rolls: 1, 2, 4, 4, 5, 6, 6 (5 Wounds).
  * **Save Roll:** Solis announces: *"5 wounds coming at Pen -1."*
  * Reavers have **SAVE 4+**. With Pen -1, they need 5+ to save. Reavers roll 5 dice: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (2 saved, 3 failed).
  * **Damage Resolution:** Each Bolter deals 1 Damage. 3 Damage total is marked off against Reaver Might (Reavers have 2 Might each = 1 model slain, 1 model reduced to 1 Might).
* **The Kinetic Suppression Trigger:**
  * Because Solis scored **7 hits** (well over the 2-hit threshold), the surviving Reavers **immediately become `[RATTLED]`** (-2" Movement and -1 to Hit)!
* **Card Tap:** Solis player rotates Crusader Squad Alpha’s card 90°. Turn passes.

### STEP 2: ABYSSAL RETALIATION & DEATH RATTLE TEST
* **Abyssal Player Activates:** Chooses the suppressed **Gore-Fiend Reavers Alpha**.
* **Action 1:** `RUSH` forward. Standard Move is 7", minus 2" from `[RATTLED]` = 5". Adds 1d6 (rolls 4) = 9" move, positioning 7" from Solis lines.
* **Action 2:** `CHARGE` Crusader Squad Alpha!
  * Charge roll: 2d6 needs 7". Rolls 3 + 5 = 8". Charge succeeds!
* **Combat Phase:**
  * 5 surviving Reavers strike with Dual Cleavers (15 attacks).
  * Attacker checks **MS 3+**, modified to 4+ by `[RATTLED]`. 8 hits land.
  * Attacker rolls 8 dice against Cleavers' flat **Wound 3+** (improved to 2+ from `[CRASHING]` on the charge!). 6 wounds caused.
  * Solis has **SAVE 3+**. With Pen -1, saves are needed on 4+. Solis rolls: 2, 3, 4, 5, 5, 6 (4 saves passed, 2 failed).
  * 2 Damage total = 1 Crusader model slain (Crusaders have 2 Might each).
* **The Death Rattle Rule in Action:**
  * The slain Crusader model was struck down before activating independently.
  * Before leaving the board, the dying Crusader executes **Death Rattle**: Makes 1 melee strike with his Combat Blade.
  * Hits on MS 3+ (rolls 5: Hit!).
  * Wounds on Blade's flat Wound 4+ (rolls 4: Wound!).
  * Reaver saves on SAVE 4+ (rolls 2: Failed!). 1 Reaver model suffers 1 Might before the Crusader drops dead.
* **Outcome:** The Crusader player traded blood for blood with zero pauses to check a Toughness chart.
